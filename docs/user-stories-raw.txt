
90-Day Fitness Quest: App User Stories
 · 
Product overview
The app turns the 90-day plan into a daily quest: follow today's workout, steps and meals, tap Finish Day, and earn up to 120 XP. These 50 user stories cover the full plan, with every exercise shown by an image.
Primary user: one person returning to the gym after a long break, starting at 106 kg with a first milestone of 96 kg and a longer-term goal of 90 kg.
In scope: workouts, steps, meals, sleep, weight and waist tracking, XP, levels, streaks and badges.
Out of scope: calorie or protein prescriptions, social leaderboards and medical advice.
Assumptions to confirm
- 
Plan length: the plan says 90 days, but its tracker covers 12 weeks (84 days). The app runs 90 days and shows the 12-week tracker within that period.
- 
Training after week 2: the plan sets 2 sets of 10–12 reps for the first two weeks only. The app keeps those targets until the user changes them or a trainer supplies a later phase.
- 
Step target from week 4: the plan says to work toward 8,000. The app sets 8,000, and the user can lower it on recovery days.
- 
Platform: a single React Native codebase for iOS, Android and web, backed by a PHP API. Windows and macOS use the web app in a browser. Phones keep working without internet at the gym.
Gamification and XP rules
Every day can earn the same 120 XP, so rest and recovery are worth as much as a gym session. XP rewards following the plan, never doing more than the plan or losing weight faster.

Epic 7: Safety and recovery
The game must never reward overtraining, under-eating or rapid weight loss.
ID
User story
Acceptance criteria
Priority
US-701
As a user, I want a Stop Exercise option so that I can respond safely to warning signs.
Lists chest pain, fainting, severe dizziness, unusual breathlessness and sharp joint pain. Advises stopping and seeking medical advice. The workout's 40 XP is still awarded.
Must
US-702
As a user, I want a Recovery Day option so that soreness does not cost me progress.
I can reduce the day's step target after significant soreness or poor recovery. Reaching the reduced target earns full step XP.
Must
US-703
As a user, I want XP capped at the plan so that the game does not push me too hard.
No XP for extra steps, extra sessions, skipped meals or kilograms lost.
Must
US-704
As a user, I want adaptation-phase limits so that I restart safely.
During weeks 1–2 the app offers no HIIT, maximum lifts or training to failure. Light, controlled weights are recommended.
Must
US-705
As a user, I want clear health guidance so that I use the plan appropriately.
States that this is a general template, results are not guaranteed, and targets should be confirmed with a doctor or registered dietitian. Warns against dehydration, starvation diets, unprescribed medication and excessive exercise.
Must
US-706
As a user, I want to log my sleep so that recovery counts toward my day.
Records hours slept against a 7–9 hour guide. Logging at least 7 hours earns 15 XP.
Must
US-707
As a user, I want to pause the plan when I am ill or injured so that I can recover without losing my streak.
A pause keeps the streak and stops reminders. It earns no XP. Resuming continues from the next plan day.
Should
